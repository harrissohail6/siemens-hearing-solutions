8 to 48 Channel Hearing Aid Prices Pakistan 2026

Direct answer: The catalog spans 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, and 48 channels. Single-unit prices range from Rs.35,000 to Rs.890,000; rechargeable pair prices range from Rs.120,000 to Rs.1,955,000. Channel count narrows the model list, but style and catalog origin tier still change the price.

Why the Same Channel Count Can Have Different Prices

A channel query is only one part of the answer. At 16 channels, Motion 1Nx is a BTE row, Pure 312 1X is RIC, Insio 1Nx is custom CIC, Silk 1X is Click CIC, and Orion 50 is rechargeable. They are different products and the rechargeable row uses a different price basis.

The catalog's channel values are seller-supplied specifications. Confirm the exact model documentation and programming features before choosing on channel count alone. A higher number is not a substitute for a hearing assessment and correct fitting.

Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

What is the price of a 16-channel hearing aid in Pakistan?

Non-rechargeable 16-channel prices are Rs.85,000 China, Rs.335,000 to Rs.385,000 Singapore, and Rs.495,000 to Rs.545,000 German per unit, depending on style. Orion 50 is Rs.120,000, Rs.350,000, or Rs.510,000 per pair.

What is the 24-channel hearing aid price in Islamabad?

Motion 3Nx is Rs.145,000, Rs.385,000, or Rs.635,000 per unit. Pure 312 3X, Insio 3Nx, and Silk 3X are Rs.145,000, Rs.435,000, or Rs.685,000 per unit. Orion 75 is priced per pair at Rs.150,000, Rs.450,000, or Rs.610,000.

How much is a 32-channel Signia hearing aid?

Motion 5Nx is Rs.135,000 China, Rs.485,000 Singapore, or Rs.710,000 German per unit. Pure 312 5X, Insio 5Nx, and Silk 5X are Rs.135,000, Rs.535,000, or Rs.760,000 per unit.

What is the 48-channel hearing aid price in Pakistan?

Motion 7Nx is Rs.285,000, Rs.595,000, or Rs.840,000 per unit. Pure 312 7X, Insio 7Nx, and Silk 7X are Rs.285,000, Rs.645,000, or Rs.890,000 per unit in China, Singapore, and German order.

What is the cheapest 8-channel hearing aid price?

Prompt BTE, Prompt CIC, and Prompt Click CIC are each listed at Rs.35,000 China, Rs.118,000 Singapore, or Rs.150,000 German per unit.

Does more channels always mean a higher price?

No. The catalog contains examples where a 32-channel China-tier model costs less than a 24-channel row. Style, origin tier, product family, and unit-versus-pair basis also affect the listed price.
