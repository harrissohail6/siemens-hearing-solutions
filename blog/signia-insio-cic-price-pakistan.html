Price Answers by Insio Model

Insio 1Nx is the 16-channel row, Insio 3Nx the 24-channel row, Insio 5Nx the 32-channel row, and Insio 7Nx the 48-channel row in this catalog. The Prompt CIC and Intuis 3 CIC provide the 8- and 12-channel entries.

The China prices match the same-tier Pure and Silk rows. Singapore and German Insio prices also match the corresponding Pure 312 X and Silk X price levels in the current catalog. The device style and fitting process differ, so a matching price does not make them interchangeable products.

Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

What is the Signia Insio 1Nx price in Pakistan?

Insio 1Nx is Rs.85,000 China, Rs.385,000 Singapore, or Rs.545,000 German per unit.

What is the Signia Insio 3Nx price in Islamabad?

Insio 3Nx is Rs.145,000 China, Rs.435,000 Singapore, or Rs.685,000 German per unit; the same base catalog is published for Islamabad.

How much is the German Insio 5Nx?

The German catalog price for Insio 5Nx is Rs.760,000 per unit. Singapore is Rs.535,000 and China is Rs.135,000.

What is the Insio 7Nx price in Pakistan?

Insio 7Nx is Rs.285,000 China, Rs.645,000 Singapore, or Rs.890,000 German per unit.

What is the cheapest CIC hearing aid in this catalog?

The Prompt CIC is the lowest-priced custom CIC row at Rs.35,000 China, Rs.118,000 Singapore, and Rs.150,000 German per unit.

Does the CIC price include two hearing aids?

No. CIC and Insio prices are listed per single unit. Ask for a written two-ear quote if you need a bilateral fitting.
