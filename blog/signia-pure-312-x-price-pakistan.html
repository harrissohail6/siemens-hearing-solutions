Which Pure Price Matches Which Search?

Searches such as “Pure 1X price in Pakistan,” “Pure 312 3X German price,” “Pure 5X Singapore price,” or “48 channel RIC hearing aid Islamabad” all refer to a specific row and origin column. The exact model name matters: 1X, 3X, 5X, and 7X are separate performance levels, and the country labels are separate catalog price tiers.

  • 16-channel Pure 312 1X: Rs.85,000 to Rs.545,000 per unit across the three columns.
  • 24-channel Pure 312 3X: Rs.145,000 to Rs.685,000 per unit.
  • 32-channel Pure 312 5X: Rs.135,000 to Rs.760,000 per unit.
  • 48-channel Pure 312 7X: Rs.285,000 to Rs.890,000 per unit.

Pure 312 X Versus Motion Nx Pricing

Pure is the catalog's RIC family, while Motion is the BTE family. At the same performance tier, the China price is currently equal, while the Pure Singapore and German rows are Rs.50,000 above the corresponding Motion row. Compare exact figures in the Motion and BTE price guide.

The family name Pure 312 X is documented by Signia. Product identity, stock, serial number, and included services should still be confirmed on the item being quoted.

Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

What is the Signia Pure 312 1X price in Pakistan?

Pure 312 1X is Rs.85,000 China, Rs.385,000 Singapore, or Rs.545,000 German per unit in the current catalog.

What is the Pure 312 3X German price in Pakistan?

Is Pure 312 X the same as Pure 312 Nx?

The Pure 312 X prices on this page are per single unit. Pure Charge & Go series prices in the rechargeable catalog are ranges quoted per pair.
