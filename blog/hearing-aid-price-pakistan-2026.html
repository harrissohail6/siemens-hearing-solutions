Use the Correct Price Basis

A common comparison error is treating a rechargeable pair price as if it were one unit, or doubling a price that is already per pair. The catalog's Prompt, Intuis, Motion, Pure 312 X, Insio, and Silk/Click prices are for one device. Orion and the Charge & Go family ranges are for two devices.

Understand the Three Catalog Columns

The site uses the commercial labels China (2nd copy), Singapore (1st copy), and German (original). These labels are reproduced for price transparency; they are not official Signia product-family names. Read the origin and authenticity guide and request written documentation for the specific item before buying.
