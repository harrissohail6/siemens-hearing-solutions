What Does “Motion 13P 1Nx” Mean?

People often search “Motion 13P 1Nx price in Pakistan” or “Siemens Motion 13 P 1Nx German price.” Official Signia material treats Motion 13 Nx and Motion 13P Nx as physical BTE forms, while 1Nx, 3Nx, 5Nx, and 7Nx identify technology or performance levels. This website's commercial catalog uses the shorter labels Motion 1Nx through Motion 7Nx.

Because 13 and 13P are not interchangeable shells, the table does not silently relabel stock. If you specifically need a Motion 13P, send the full label from the box, invoice, or existing device and ask for written confirmation. See the official Motion 13P Nx technical sheet for the manufacturer's naming.

How to Compare the BTE Rows

  • 8 and 12 channels: Prompt, Intuis 3, and Intuis 4.2 occupy the lower channel tiers in this catalog.
  • 16 channels: Motion 1Nx is the catalog's 16-channel BTE row.
  • 24, 32, and 48 channels: Motion 3Nx, 5Nx, and 7Nx are the corresponding rows.

Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

What is the Signia Motion 1Nx price in Pakistan?

The catalog price for one Signia Motion 1Nx is Rs.85,000 for China (2nd copy), Rs.335,000 for Singapore (1st copy), and Rs.495,000 for German (original).

What is the German Motion 13P 1Nx price in Pakistan?

No exact Motion 13P 1Nx price is published in this catalog. The separate German Motion 1Nx row is Rs.495,000 per unit, but that is not a confirmed 13P quote; verify the physical 13P form on the box, invoice, and offered stock first.

How much is a 16-channel BTE hearing aid in Pakistan or Islamabad?

The 16-channel Motion 1Nx is Rs.85,000 China, Rs.335,000 Singapore, or Rs.495,000 German per unit. The rechargeable Orion 50 is also listed as 16 channel but is priced separately per pair.

What are the Motion 3Nx, 5Nx, and 7Nx German prices?

The German catalog prices per unit are Rs.635,000 for Motion 3Nx, Rs.710,000 for Motion 5Nx, and Rs.840,000 for Motion 7Nx.

Are Signia Motion prices the same in Islamabad and across Pakistan?

This website publishes one base catalog for Islamabad and Pakistan delivery. The final total can still vary with stock, fitting, earmold, warranty, delivery, and any quantity agreement, so request a written quote.

Are BTE prices listed per unit or per pair?

Prompt, Intuis, and Motion BTE prices are per single unit. Do not compare them directly with the rechargeable section until you account for rechargeable prices being quoted per pair.
