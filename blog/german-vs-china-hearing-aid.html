German vs Singapore vs China Hearing Aid Prices Pakistan

August 19, 2026Origin & Pricing Guide8 min read

Direct answer: This website's catalog uses three commercial price columns: China (2nd copy), Singapore (1st copy), and German (original). Non-rechargeable prices run from Rs.35,000 to Rs.285,000 in the China column, Rs.118,000 to Rs.645,000 in Singapore, and Rs.150,000 to Rs.890,000 in German. Rechargeable pair ranges extend higher.

Important terminology: “2nd copy,” “1st copy,” and “original” are labels used by this seller's catalog; they are not official Signia product-family names. Before buying, ask for the exact model label, serial number, invoice wording, origin documentation, warranty issuer, return terms, and written statement of authenticity.

How to Verify What You Are Being Quoted

  1. Get the full model, platform, performance level, and physical form in writing.
  2. Ask whether the amount is per unit or per pair and whether fitting or accessories are included.
  3. Ask the seller to state whether the device is manufacturer-original, refurbished, re-cased, copied, or otherwise sourced.
  4. Ask who provides the warranty, its duration, exclusions, and where a claim is handled.
  5. Match the label and serial information on the physical device, packaging, invoice, and warranty card.

For exact row-by-row prices, use the complete Pakistan price guide. These steps are more reliable than assuming a country label alone proves authenticity or manufacturing origin.

Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

What is the price of a German original hearing aid in Pakistan?

In this Signia catalog, German-column non-rechargeable models range from Rs.150,000 to Rs.890,000 per unit. Rechargeable German rows range from Rs.510,000 to Rs.1,955,000 per pair.

What is the Singapore hearing aid price in Pakistan?

Singapore-column non-rechargeable prices range from Rs.118,000 to Rs.645,000 per unit. Rechargeable Singapore prices range from Rs.350,000 to Rs.790,000 per pair.

What is the China hearing aid price in Pakistan?

China-column non-rechargeable prices range from Rs.35,000 to Rs.285,000 per unit. Rechargeable China prices range from Rs.120,000 to Rs.280,000 per pair.

Is a Singapore 1st copy an official Signia product tier?

The phrase 'Singapore (1st copy)' is this seller catalog's commercial label, not an official Signia family name. Request written origin, authenticity, serial, warranty, and invoice evidence for the exact device.

Does 'German original' prove the hearing aid is authentic?

A catalog label alone is not proof. Verify the physical model and serial, invoice description, warranty issuer and terms, packaging, and seller documentation before payment.

Why do China, Singapore, and German prices differ?

The catalog applies different prices to its three source or grade labels. The labels alone do not explain every difference, so ask the seller to document product condition, origin, authenticity, warranty, included services, and return terms.
